Our Story

Born in the Mountains, Brewed for Everyone

Nestled in the heart of the Colorado Rockies, Two Leaves and a Bud was founded on a simple belief: tea should be extraordinary. We source only the top two leaves and a bud from each tea plant — the most flavorful, nutrient-rich part of the harvest.

Every cup we craft reflects our commitment to organic farming, sustainable practices, and the kind of warmth that turns strangers into friends.
